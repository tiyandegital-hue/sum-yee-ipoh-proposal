Arrival & Etiquette

Navigating to Taman Canning

Located in one of Ipoh’s most beloved green heritage quarters. Street parking bays sit along the peaceful perimeter of Jalan Canning Estate, right in front of the salon gate.

Street Parking & Accessibility

Street parking is available along Jalan Canning Estate. Arrive a few minutes early so your consultation can start on time.

For patrons observing hijab, our dedicated private room guarantees complete separation from public areas. Please request the private suite when you book so we can reserve the space exclusively for you.
