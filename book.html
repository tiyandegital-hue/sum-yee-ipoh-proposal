Frequently Addressed Booking Protocols

Booking, answered.

Why is booking handled via message & consultation rather than rigid bots?

Hair texture, chemical density and scalp condition are unique to every individual. A short conversation with a senior stylist beats a rigid form — the consultation is where your price and plan are agreed.

How do I ensure the Muslimah Private Suite is reserved for my appointment?

Keep the “Muslimah Private Enclave Suite” toggle active in Step 2 above. It is added to your message draft, and the front desk will reserve the partitioned room for your visit.

What is your arrival protocol?

Please arrive a few minutes before your time slot so your consultation can start on time. If you are running late, call the salon — the desk will advise on the next available slot.

Why is there no price list on the site?

Every price is agreed on consultation, because hair density, length and past colour history change the work involved. No flat rates, no hidden upcharges — the consultation is free of obligation.

Which branch should I choose?

Canning Garden is the flagship and academy atelier, with the private Muslimah suite and garden setting. Pasir Puteh is the neighbourhood branch for precision cuts and express care. Both share one standard of craft.
